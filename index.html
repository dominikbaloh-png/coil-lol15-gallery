SDG 15

UN Life on Land Field Guide

Target 15.1 & 15.2 — Sustainable Forests & Ecosystems Conserve and restore terrestrial and inland freshwater ecosystems. Planting Native Forest (🌲) increases passive Eco-Energy generation and shelters wildlife.
Target 15.3 — Combat Desertification Restore degraded land and soil affected by drought and erosion. Use Wetland Oasis (💧) to convert barren desert back into fertile soil and protect neighboring tiles from wildfires.
Target 15.5 & 15.7 — Halt Biodiversity Loss & Poaching Protect threatened species and stop illegal logging or invasive weeds. Deploy Eco-Ranger (🚒) to clear threats, and build Bio-Corridor (🦌) between forests to attract rare wildlife!
